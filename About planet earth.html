Planet Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life.

Facts about Earth

  • Earth has a diameter of about 12,742 km.
  • It has one natural satellite, the Moon.
  • Earth's atmosphere is composed mainly of nitrogen and oxygen.
  • The planet has a diverse range of ecosystems and climates.

Steps to Protect Earth

  1. Reduce, reuse, and recycle to minimize waste.
  2. Conserve water and energy resources.
  3. Plant trees and support reforestation efforts.
  4. Support policies and initiatives that protect the environment.

Incredible Natural Wonders

  • The Grand Canyon in the United States.
  • The Great Barrier Reef in Australia.
  • Mount Everest in the Himalayas.
  • The Amazon Rainforest in South America.

Key Terms

Atmosphere
The layer of gases surrounding Earth, essential for life.
Biosphere
The global sum of all ecosystems, including all living beings and their relationships.
Climate Change
Long-term alterations in temperature, precipitation, and other atmospheric conditions on Earth.
Geology
The study of the Earth's physical structure, substance, history, and processes.
