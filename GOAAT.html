Greatest Olympic Athletes of All Time

Discover the most remarkable athletes who have left an indelible mark on the history of the Olympic Games. From record-breaking performances to inspiring stories of perseverance, these athletes have captivated audiences worldwide.

Athlete Sport Olympic Achievements
Usain Bolt Track and Field 8-time Olympic gold medalist, world record holder in 100m and 200m sprints.
Michael Phelps Swimming 23-time Olympic gold medalist, the most decorated Olympian of all time.
Simone Biles Gymnastics 4-time Olympic gold medalist, known for her extraordinary skills and routines.
Carl Lewis Track and Field 9-time Olympic gold medalist, renowned for his dominance in long jump and sprinting events.
Larisa Latynina Gymnastics 18-time Olympic medalist, holding the record for the most Olympic medals won by a female athlete.
Mark Spitz Swimming 9-time Olympic gold medalist, known for his exceptional performances in the 1972 Munich Olympics.
Paavo Nurmi Track and Field 9-time Olympic gold medalist, known as the "Flying Finn" for his dominance in long-distance running.
