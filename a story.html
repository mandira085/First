The Lion and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a lion who lived in the jungle. One day, while he was sleeping, a little mouse ran over his paw. The lion woke up and caught the mouse in his paw.

The mouse begged for mercy and promised to help the lion one day. The lion laughed at the idea but let the mouse go. A few days later, the lion was caught in a hunter's net. The mouse heard the lion's roar and came to help. The mouse gnawed through the ropes and freed the lion.

The lion was grateful and realized that even the smallest creatures can be of great help. From that day on, the lion and the mouse became good friends.
