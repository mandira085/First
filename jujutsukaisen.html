Jujutsu Kaisen

Jujutsu Kaisen is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Gege Akutami. It was serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump from March 2018 to March 2020, with its chapters collected into 24 tankōbon volumes by Shueisha.

The story follows high school student Yuji Itadori as he joins a secret organization of Jujutsu Sorcerers to eliminate a powerful Curse named Ryomen Sukuna, of whom Yuji becomes the host.

The series has been adapted into an anime television series produced by MAPPA, which premiered in October 2020. The anime has received critical acclaim for its animation, fight sequences, and character development.

Jujutsu Kaisen has become one of the most popular manga and anime series in recent years, with a large fanbase worldwide. It has also inspired a feature film, Jujutsu Kaisen 0, which was released in 2021 and serves as a prequel to the main story.
