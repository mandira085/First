Dragon Ball

Dragon Ball is a Japanese manga series written by Akira Toriyama and illustrated by Shotaro Ishinomori. The story follows Goku, a young boy with a monkey tail, as he embarks on a journey to find the seven Dragon Balls, which can grant any wish when assembled.

Main Characters

  • Goku

  • Vegeta

  • Gohan

  • Piccolo

  • Frieza

Plot Summary

The series begins with Goku living a simple life until he meets Bulma, a brilliant inventor who is searching for the Dragon Balls. Together, they embark on an adventure to collect all seven balls and wish for their hearts' desires.

As the story progresses, Goku faces increasingly powerful enemies, including the evil Saiyan prince Vegeta and the tyrannical Frieza. Each battle pushes Goku to his limits and beyond, showcasing his growth as a warrior.

Anime Adaptation

The anime adaptation of Dragon Ball has become a cultural phenomenon, known for its action-packed battles and memorable characters. The series has been praised for its animation quality and engaging storyline.
